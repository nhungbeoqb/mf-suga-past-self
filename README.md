Entry for the make.fun bounty
[Two Lines to Your Past Self](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca):
two lines — a warning to my past self about one specific moment, and one
sentence on why it mattered.

## The entry

> Answer on the third ring that Thursday in March — it's Dad, and he never calls twice.
>
> You let it ring a fourth time, and the next voice on the line was a nurse holding his phone.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes the two lines to
`/output/entry.txt`. The text is fixed and deterministic: 179 characters,
two lines, one sentence each.

## Why this moment

The judge weighs how concrete the moment is and how clearly the second
line traces cause and effect. This entry names a moment you can picture —
the third ring, a Thursday in March, a father who never calls twice — and
the second line shows the consequence in one image: one unanswered ring,
and the last conversation never happened.
